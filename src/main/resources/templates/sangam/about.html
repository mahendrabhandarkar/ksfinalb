We modernised the rishta — without losing its soul.

"Sangam" means confluence. We built this platform so two families could meet the way they always have — with introductions, patience and elders in the room — while the tedious parts: verification, search, horoscope matching, are handled with care by people and honest software.

What we refuse to compromise on

Verification first

Government-ID and live selfie checks before any profile goes live — re-verified randomly through membership.

Family in the loop

Up to three linked family logins and family-moderated conversations, by design not as an afterthought.

Privacy by default

Photos blurred, contacts hidden, nothing sold — ever. Your data leaves our servers only when you delete it.

No magic algorithms

Every ranking rule is explainable and adjustable. If we can't explain why someone was shown to you, we don't show them.

Twenty-seven years, one promise

From a single Mumbai office with a filing cabinet of bio-datas to 26 cities — the promise has never changed: verified people, real intentions, patient guidance.

  1. 1998Founded above a jewellery shop in Dadar. 40 hand-typed bio-datas, 3 marriages in year one.
  2. 2005The verification desk opens — every profile checked by a human before going live, a first in Indian matrimony.
  3. 2014Mobile apps launch with family-linked logins; parents and children finally share one search.
  4. 2020Family-moderated video introductions — pioneered during a year when weddings went intimate.
  5. 2025Every page of this site ships under a strict Content-Security-Policy — security as a member benefit, not an afterthought.
