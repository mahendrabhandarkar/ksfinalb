Our Story

Where tradition meets technology, love finds a way

Founded in 2018 in Mumbai, Vivaah Royal began with one belief: every person deserves a match made with dignity, privacy and family blessings — regardless of ability, ability to pay, or geography.

Our mission

To make the search for a life partner safe, joyful and accessible for every Indian — combining the wisdom of elders with the intelligence of technology, so no heart settles for less than 'right'.

Our vision

A world where finding your life partner is as respectful as it is exciting — where profiles are authentic, consent is absolute, and the platform serves every member, including those using assistive technologies.

Our values

Privacy before profit. Authenticity before volume. Accessibility before aesthetics. Family involvement before algorithms. And celebrations — always celebrations — before everything else.
