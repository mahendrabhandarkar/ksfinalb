Seven golden safety rules

  1. Never share OTPs, bank details or transfer money — genuine matches will never ask.
  2. Keep conversations on the platform until you feel confident.
  3. Video call before meeting in person.
  4. Meet in public places; inform family about your plans.
  5. Verify family details through trusted common references.
  6. Report suspicious behaviour — our team responds within 24 hours.
  7. Trust your instincts. If something feels wrong, it probably is.

Our accessibility statement

Vivaah Royal is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We continually improve the user experience for everyone and apply the relevant accessibility standards, aiming to conform with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

Measures we have taken include:

  • Semantic HTML with proper landmarks, headings and labels on every page.
  • Full keyboard operability, with visible focus indicators and skip-to-content links.
  • Text resizing controls (A− / A / A+) and a high-contrast mode in the navigation bar.
  • Colour palette tested for WCAG AA contrast — deep maroon text on ivory backgrounds.
  • Screen-reader friendly components: ARIA live regions for dynamic results, alerts and toasts.
  • Respect for the user's prefers-reduced-motion setting across animations.
  • Descriptive alternative text for meaningful images; decorative visuals hidden from assistive technology.

If you face any accessibility barrier, write to access@vivaahroyal.example — our accessibility team responds within 2 working days.
